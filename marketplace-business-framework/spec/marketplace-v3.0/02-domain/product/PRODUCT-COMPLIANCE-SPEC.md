# Product Compliance SPEC

ComplianceRequirement comes from category/region/policy:
- license/certificate
- age restriction
- shelf life
- origin
- safety certification
- brand proof
- prohibited/restricted product checks

`ProductComplianceSnapshot` freezes requirement versions and evidence references used for a published Offer version.

Status:
PENDING / PASS / REVIEW / FAIL / EXPIRED.

FAIL/EXPIRED may block new sale; governance may force Offer BLOCKED.
