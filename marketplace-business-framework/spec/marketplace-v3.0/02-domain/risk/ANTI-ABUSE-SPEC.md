# Anti-Abuse SPEC

## Buyer Abuse
- multi-account coupon farming
- flash bot
- refund abuse
- fake review
- stolen account/payment abuse

## Seller Abuse
- brush orders
- brush reviews
- fake shipment
- seller-buyer collusion
- counterfeit/restricted goods
- abnormal refund manipulation
- payout anomaly
